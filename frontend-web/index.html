Agentic Architecture

How PlanWise AI Works

A stateful planning agent designed to avoid generic, hallucinated travel recommendations.

1

Understands Your Request

A hosted AI model extracts your destination, dates, budget and preferences. No destination? It uses your current location.

2

Grounds It in Live Data

The place is geocoded and real hotels, restaurants, sights and transport hubs are pulled live from OpenStreetMap.

3

Plans & Verifies

The AI composes the itinerary; deterministic code then checks dates, day counts and budget totals, and flags anything unverified.
